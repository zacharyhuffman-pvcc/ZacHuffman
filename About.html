About Me

Hello! My name is Zachary Huffman, I'm a 28-year-old from Harrisonburg, Virginia who is currently looking to breaking into the IT world. My path into technology was a bit unconventional. I originally planned to study Computer Science in college, but a part-time job after high school unexpectedly turned into a full-fledged career. I began in a management role at the then-small cookie company, Insomnia Cookies, gaining hands-on experience in corporate operations and traveling to cities like Philadelphia, Atlanta, and several locations in Florida.

Before the COVID-19 pandemic, I chose to leave this role and return to school to pursue a degree in Business. While I excelled in my studies, the pandemic provided perspective on what truly drives me professionally. During this period, I also paused my education to support my father through serious health challenges. Although he ultimately passed away, the experience strengthened my resilience, determination, and focus on what I value most in my life.

I am currently completing my Associate’s degree in Cloud Computing as an honors student, maintaining a near-perfect GPA. I actively participate in a coding club to sharpen my programming skills and collaborate with like-minded peers. In addition, I hold several IT certifications and am passionate about building a career in IT, which I find both intellectually stimulating and rewarding. My long-term goal is to pursue a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science.

Outside of technology, I pursue photography with a focus on weddings, portraits, and especially car and street photography. I also enjoy working on my own vehicle, tuning and modifying it to improve performance. These creative and technical pursuits challenge me in different ways and bring me genuine satisfaction.
